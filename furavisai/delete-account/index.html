Furavis AI — Account Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your Furavis AI account and associated data using either of the methods below.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open the Furavis AI app
  2. Tap the Profile tab
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted

Option 2 — Email request

Send a deletion request to support@zentryxinteractive.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All pet profiles and associated health data
  • Health history, symptom checks, and diagnoses
  • Chat history with Sage
  • Uploaded photos and documents
  • Subscription information (active subscriptions must be cancelled separately through Google Play or the App Store)

Retention period

All data is permanently deleted within 30 days of the deletion request. Anonymized, non-identifiable usage data may be retained for analytics purposes.
